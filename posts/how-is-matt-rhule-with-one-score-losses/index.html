Where does Nebraska sit for one-score-losses?

The Nebraska Cornhuskers have had quite the history of one-score-losses.

They have recently been thrown quite some shade for their ability to finish out close games with Head Coach Matt Rhule. These one-score-losses aren’t easy for the Husker fans. Given their slight bit of hope throughout the game, the Huskers can’t seem to hold on and secure the win as the fourth quarter comes to an end. Matt Rhule isn’t the only coach blamed of these happenings, Scott Frost -prior coach to Rhule- had his fair share of blame before he was fired in 2022. But who’s the culprit?

library(tidyverse)
library(ggbeeswarm)
library(gt)

gamelogs18 <- read_csv("gamelogs2018.csv") |> mutate(game_label = paste("Week", week, "vs", opponent, season))
gamelogs19 <- read_csv("gamelogs2019.csv") |> mutate(game_label = paste("Week", week, "vs", opponent, season))
gamelogs20 <- read_csv("gamelogs2020.csv") |> mutate(game_label = paste("Week", week, "vs", opponent, season))
gamelogs21 <- read_csv("gamelogs2021.csv") |> mutate(game_label = paste("Week", week, "vs", opponent, season))
gamelogs22 <- read_csv("gamelogs2022.csv") |> mutate(game_label = paste("Week", week, "vs", opponent, season))
gamelogs23 <- read_csv("gamelogs2023.csv") |> mutate(game_label = paste("Week", week, "vs", opponent, season))
gamelogs24 <- read_csv("gamelogs2024.csv") |> mutate(game_label = paste("Week", week, "vs", opponent, season))
gamelogs25 <- read_csv("gamelogs2025.csv") |> mutate(game_label = paste("Week", week, "vs", opponent, season))
gamelogs26 <- read_csv("gamelogs2026.csv") |> mutate(game_label = paste("Week", week, "vs", opponent, season))

gamelogs <- bind_rows(gamelogs18, gamelogs19, gamelogs20, gamelogs21, gamelogs22, gamelogs23, gamelogs24, gamelogs25, gamelogs26)

onescoregames <- gamelogs |>
filter(conference == "Big Ten") |> 
  mutate(
point_difference = points - points_allowed 
) |>
  group_by(school, season)  |>
summarize(
one_score_losses = n()
) |>
mutate(
coach = case_when(
season <= 2022 ~ "Frost",
season >= 2023 ~ "Rhule"
)
) |>
group_by(school, coach, season) |>
summarize(
avg_losses = mean(one_score_losses)
) |>
pivot_wider(
  names_from = coach,
  values_from =avg_losses
)

nebraska <- gamelogs |>
  filter(
    school == "Nebraska") |>
  mutate(
    turnover_margin = turnovers_allowed - turnovers,
    point_difference = points - points_allowed,
    result = case_when(
      point_difference > 0 ~ "Win",
      point_difference < 0 ~ "Loss"
    ),
    coach = case_when(
      season <= 2022 ~ "Scott Frost",
      season >= 2023 ~ "Matt Rhule"
    )
  )

nebraska |> 
  mutate(
    point_difference = points - points_allowed 
) |>
  filter(abs(point_difference) <= 8) |> 
group_by(season) |>
  summarize(
    TotalGames = n(),
    Wins = sum(points > points_allowed), 
    Losses = sum(points < points_allowed)
  ) |> 
  mutate(
    WinningPCT = Wins/TotalGames
  ) |> 
  gt() |>
  cols_label(

    title ="Does Nebraska seal the deal in games down to the wire?",

  ) |> tab_style(
    style = cell_text(color = "black", weight = "bold", align = "left"),
    locations = cells_title("title")
  ) |> tab_style(
    style = cell_text(color = "black", align = "left"),
    locations = cells_title("subtitle")

  tab_source_note(

  ) |>

     locations = cells_column_labels(columns = everything()),

       cell_borders(sides = "bottom", weight = px(3)),

     )
   ) |>
  opt_row_striping() |>

If you talk to a Gen X or a Millennial, they will laugh. They won’t just laugh, they will punish you. Not because you did something wrong, but because you didn’t get to see the absolute unit of a team Nebraska was before your day of birth. Nebraska has claimed 5 national championships from the years 1970-1997. Fans will forever mourn the loss of the team subject to change. But why can’t we just slither our way back in time like the scientist in The Time Machine, and bring back a piece of victory?

Nebraska obviously isn’t the only team in the Big Ten Conference who holds onto one-score losses. Each team has had their fair share. But what makes Nebraska stand out when looking at these statistics?

The 2026 season is currently 5-4 overall 3 of which are one-score games. During those contests the Huskers are 0-3 on one-score games.
